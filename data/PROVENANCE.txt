  Medium: potato dextrose broth in shake flasks
  orange_pigment_yield_g_l: g/L equivalent of quinizarin (absorbance at 470 nm)
  red_pigment_yield_g_l: g/L equivalent of red yeast rice pigment (500 nm)
  biomass_g_l: dry biomass weight (g/L)
